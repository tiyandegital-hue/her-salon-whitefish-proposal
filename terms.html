Using this site

You’re welcome to browse, share links, and send booking requests. Don’t scrape the site aggressively, don’t pretend to be the salon, and don’t submit anyone else’s personal details through the form.

Bookings

Real appointments are made through the salon’s Schedulicity booking page and are subject to the salon’s own scheduling and cancellation terms. The sample request form on this site creates no booking.

Content

Copy and concept artwork on this site belong to the salon. The artwork is original concept art drawn for this proposal — not photography of real guests or the real studio.

Prices and hours

Any prices or hours marked “sample” are placeholders for design purposes. The salon’s confirmed prices and hours always win.

Changes

These terms may be updated as the site grows up from proposal to production. Continued use means you accept the current version.
